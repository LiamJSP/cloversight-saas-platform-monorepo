## Information we collect {#collect}

We process account and invited-team identity and contact information; organization and tax-jurisdiction details; user watching-region preferences; billing transaction and payment-provider references; watcher URLs, configuration, AWS check regions, thresholds, selectors, phrases, and recipient destinations; captured page text, screenshots, comparison results, generated reports, and alert responses; support correspondence; and security, action, and operational logs.

The subscription billing flow uses Stripe-hosted Checkout and has no CLOVERSIGHT payment-card entry fields. Captures, free-text notes and inbound email can contain unsolicited sensitive information; do not submit payment card credentials through watcher targets, notes, support or email. A monitored page may contain personal or confidential information selected by the customer; customers control which authorized URLs they submit and which page regions they exclude.

## How we use information {#use}

We use information to create and secure accounts, run requested monitoring, compare captures, deliver and reconcile alerts, process subscriptions, calculate taxes and credits, provide support, prevent abuse, investigate reliability and security events, maintain audit records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

CLOVERSIGHT does not sell personal information or use monitored page content for third-party advertising.

## Service providers and transfers {#providers}

CLOVERSIGHT uses infrastructure and specialized providers to deliver the service, including Amazon Web Services for hosting, identity, storage, logging, and email transport; Stripe for subscription billing; and Twilio when SMS delivery is enabled. These providers process information under their own service terms and privacy commitments.

Providers may process information in Canada, the United States, or other jurisdictions where they operate. Information can therefore be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

## Retention and deletion {#retention}

Customer-visible monitoring history follows the plan's 7-, 30-, 90-, 180-, or 365-day horizon. Scale is not available for self-service activation; confirm storage and retention terms with support before purchasing. Physical storage cleanup is asynchronous and includes processing buffers. Current comparison evidence and approved baseline records needed for monitoring continuity can be retained beyond the ordinary history window. Generated HTML and PDF report downloads expire seven days after the report request. Operational logs and support records are retained for periods appropriate to security, reliability, and support needs. Billing and audit records may be retained longer where required for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

Scheduling account closure disables the account and pauses monitoring immediately. Operational data is scheduled for deletion after the configured closure grace period. Unresolved payments, refunds, or an uncanceled Stripe subscription can delay deletion; records that must be retained remain restricted. Closure requests immediate subscription cancellation. The portal shows the request status and confirmed effective date. Billing records needed to reconcile payments, refunds, or cancellation are retained until the outcome is resolved; reopening does not restart billing.

## Security {#security}

We use access controls, encryption in transit, encrypted AWS storage, role separation, masked administrative views, bounded capture workers, and audit logging. No system is completely secure, so customers should monitor only authorized pages and avoid pages containing unnecessary sensitive information. Ignored selectors filter text and screenshots after the page is fetched; they are not a guarantee that excluded content is never processed.

## Your choices and rights {#choices}

You can update account information and schedule account closure in the customer portal. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, deletion, or information about our handling of your personal information. Some records may be retained where permitted or required by law.

CLOVERSIGHT uses browser storage for authentication and service operation, and IndexedDB for the convenience of local watcher-draft recovery. Recovery drafts expire after seven days when next accessed. This browser attempts to clear drafts for the current account and viewer when signing out or scheduling account closure. You can clear a waiting draft in the Developer desk or remove this site's browser storage; local drafts are separate from server data. Managed sign-in and checkout have their own browser technologies.

## Contact and changes {#contact}

For privacy questions or requests, email [support@cloversight.app](mailto:support@cloversight.app?subject=Privacy%20request). Please do not include passwords, authentication tokens, full payment card numbers, or private capture content.

We may update this notice as the service, providers, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by the date at the top of this page and, when appropriate, by an in-product or account notice.
