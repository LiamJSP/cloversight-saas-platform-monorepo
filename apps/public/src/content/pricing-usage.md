heading: Plan for the checks you need.
---

The fastest cadence is a minimum interval, not unlimited monitoring at that rate. For a 30-day month, budget about 21,600 credits for one watcher checked every two minutes in one region, or 108,000 credits in five regions.

Scheduled checks and Check Now share the same credit allowance and minimum interval. Edits, pause/resume, and undo do not reset those limits. Starts are spread automatically, so exact wall-clock times are not guaranteed. First checks for a full Essentials account can spread across about an hour.

Monitoring pauses when credits run out. Included credits do not roll over, and prepaid packs and automatic overage are not currently offered. The portal shows your current usage window; a verified paid renewal starts a fresh allowance. Platform recovery uses bounded retries and can also place checks on hold after repeated failures.
