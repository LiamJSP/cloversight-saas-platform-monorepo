# Regional globe third-party notices

The shipped browser bundle derives from the supplied Web Watcher globe prototype and includes Three.js (MIT), d3-geo (ISC), d3-array (ISC), internmap (ISC), topojson-client (ISC), and world-atlas (ISC). The coastline data in world-atlas derives from Natural Earth (public domain). Exact versions and integrity hashes are pinned in the root package-lock.json. The associated licenses are included in `licenses/`.

The prototype used Astro and Svelte for its demonstration shell; the integrated portal does not distribute or use those frameworks.
