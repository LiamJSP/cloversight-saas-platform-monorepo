# Known limitations and external release gates

The current production gates are in [the 0.5.16 assessment](security-production-review-0.5.16.md). Historical readiness records remain available.

- The owner must certify CloudFormation deployment/rollback, permissions, DNS, regional queues/buckets, browser capture, and account erasure in staging.
- The source and generated documentation describe configured behavior, not live certification. Run the release/deployment gates before promotion; this source review does not establish an isolation, payment, accessibility, or cost guarantee.
- All three source dependency trees now have registry-resolved locks and pass npm audit at review time. Actual Docker/OS/browser scanning and live AWS acceptance remain outstanding; see the 0.5.16 assessment.
- Browser-process reuse is intentionally deferred. The proven Lambda path requires a persistent/default Playwright context, disposable profile, and capture-scoped pinned egress proxy. Reuse should be introduced only in a supervised warm worker after security and stability testing.
- The queues support a future ECS/Fargate consumer, but release 0.5.0 deploys Lambda consumers only. Lambda still launches Chromium per capture, although warm environments can reuse module loading and extracted binaries.
- Capture reads, watcher edits, direct evidence authorization, scheduling, membership lookup, and capture mutations are partitioned/indexed; routine capture transactions do not load retained history. Billing, notification, report quota admission, closure, maintenance, broad bootstrap, and steward compatibility commands still use the global coherent transaction adapter or an account-wide compatibility view and remain a sustained-throughput limit.
- DynamoDB `LookupIndex` is eventually consistent; strong direct pointer reads protect current watcher/job/snapshot/alert values and deterministic temporary regional results on the capture hot path. Broader customer lists can lag briefly after a write.
- Upgrading an existing table adds `LookupIndex` and `IdentityIndex` in separate CloudFormation operations because DynamoDB accepts only one new GSI per table update. The deploy script owns that sequence; keep the maintenance window active until both phases complete.
- Scale's 1 TB included-storage limit and paid storage overage are not metered. Scale is blocked from self-serve checkout.
- Prepaid credit packs, automatic/capped overage billing, annual pricing, and SMS usage billing are not implemented. Included credits pause monitoring when exhausted and do not roll over.
- Existing paid subscriptions on the pre-0.5.0 CAD Price IDs require an explicit Stripe/internal migration. The application keeps their retired entitlements and exact CAD renewal reconciliation available, but blocks their self-serve checkout/plan-change path. Do not archive an old Stripe Price until every bound subscription is migrated or cancelled.
- Regional account erasure discovers configured Regions. Follow the lifecycle runbook before removing a regional stack/config entry so retained orphan buckets are not missed.
- The 128 KB default S3 lifecycle transition floor means small text/metadata objects remain in Standard; this is intentional.
- Authorized evidence downloads use short-lived regional S3 pre-signed URLs. A CloudFront OAC/signed-URL layer is deferred; the API/Lambda binary proxy cost is already removed, but CloudFront-specific delivery savings and centralized edge controls are not included yet.
- Daily history packfiles, Deep Archive restoration, dormant-account hibernation, and report-result caching remain deferred. Release 0.5.0 applies content-addressed monthly evidence and lifecycle tiers without introducing an asynchronous archive-restore workflow.
- Production deployments require steward MFA ON. Existing staging/beta choices remain explicit; enabling production MFA still requires owner-tested enrollment, recovery and role separation before exposing customer/payment-support data.
- PayPal and Paddle remain fail-closed stubs.
- Legal/tax pages and finance policy require current professional review. SES, Stripe Tax, Twilio, accessibility, load, penetration, disaster-recovery, and provider flows require live staging certification.
- Verify Stripe test-mode paid, incomplete, async, failed renewal, out-of-band paid, and past-due sequences with real webhook payloads before beta access. Existing paid accounts missing a current, valid Stripe billing period fail closed until reconciled; confirm their Stripe subscription period and account records during rollout.
- Verify two unrelated customer accounts against every customer API, snapshot image URL, report URL, check-status endpoint, and recipient search. Also verify keyboard focus/drag behavior and contrast in supported desktop browsers. This release includes source-level fixes but no live assistive-device or full WCAG conformance certification.

## October 10 remediation boundaries

The [complete audit change register](audit-change-register-2026-10-10.md) is the remaining-work source of truth. In particular:

- Account-wide AWS Budgets and approximate queue admission are safeguards, not a near-live spend ledger or a dollar shutdown governor. Metering physical attempts, reconciliation, rolling 24-hour liabilities, freshness checks, independent financial roles, permits and safe hold actuators remain deferred. Credits are not an AWS spend ceiling.
- Maintenance leases and bounded deletion stages prevent several lost-work and false-completion cases. They do not replace indexed backlog queries, independent operational outboxes, confirmed SES delivery feedback, multi-region rate permits or a restore/replay tombstone workflow. Closure and reopening now use bounded resumable batches, including 500-watcher accounts; they still use the global compatibility store instead of indexed account work queues.
- Erasure covers operational records, resolved Cognito identities, unsubscribe links and versions in currently configured evidence buckets. Historical buckets, backups, provider-side records, tax/security retention policy and late object-write races require an approved inventory/replay workflow. Stripe renewal cancellation and immediate cancellation on closure are automated in 0.5.19 with durable intents, provider readback and bounded retries. Ambiguous old Checkout requests, binding mismatches, failed confirmations and unresolved financial records still require operator reconciliation; live Stripe/AWS acceptance remains required. Reopening never restarts billing, and a new subscription after cancellation is currently support-assisted. See [cancellation behavior](cancellation-0.5.19.md).
- Own-profile JSON export is deliberately limited to the requesting member and their own profile/membership. It is not a comprehensive person-level GDPR access or portability workflow. The HTML report companion preserves the report model's Unicode and semantics; the existing PDF remains untagged and the report model retains a 5,000-result/20-incident scope limit.
- Source-level focus, contrast, keyboard, error/status and draft-lifecycle changes require rendered keyboard, assistive-technology, contrast and reflow acceptance across all audit matrix criteria. No WCAG, PCI or GDPR certification is claimed.
- The browser egress proxy enforces cumulative bytes and connection/request bounds during transfer, but the renderer still shares a privilege boundary with its capture worker. Credentialless broker separation remains deferred.
