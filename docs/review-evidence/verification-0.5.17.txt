Scope of new tests
  Exact six-plan matrix; stale index and concurrent final-slot claims;
  deletion/undo counters; administrative reconciliation without ledger reset;
  preserved cadence/evidence/recovery; paced 250-watcher admission; atomic
  credits and attempts; manual queue/local rejection; cancellation charging;
  known infrastructure refund exactly once; finite refunded-attempt budget;
  temporary recipients; GSM/Unicode SMS and reply instructions; invitation
  opt-outs, expiry, destination cooldown and daily budgets; account request
  and daily mutation limits; plan cycling and consumed-credit downgrades;
  paid renewal replay after plan change; raw subscription status isolation;
  pre-charge entity/byte rejection; bounded large-account renewal resumption.

Limits
  Cohort tests use deterministic fake persistence/providers, not live AWS.
  No cloud deployment/account inspection, Docker/browser acceptance, actual
  messages/payments, container scan or restore drill was performed.
  Node 22 verification remains a CI/owner gate; this workspace used Node 24.
  Provider retries are bounded and metered, not an exactly-once guarantee.
  No physical dollar or retained-byte meter was added. See the abuse review
  for signup, renderer, global-read, retention and lifecycle limitations.
